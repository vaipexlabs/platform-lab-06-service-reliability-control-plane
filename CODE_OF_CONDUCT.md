# Community Code of Conduct

Vaipex Labs is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically
constructive open-source community.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat participants with respect and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback with enough context to help others succeed.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `vaipex.labs@gmail.com`. Include relevant
context and links when safe to do so. Reports will be reviewed confidentially
and handled according to their severity and the safety of those involved.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or take other proportionate action when behavior violates these expectations.
